Moo Logic Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026　|　Effective date: November 4, 2026

Moo Logic ("the Game") is provided by us (the "Developer"). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the Game handles (collects, uses, stores, and shares) information. Please read it carefully before using the Game. By using the Game, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 No account required

The Game does not require registration or login, and we do not actively collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

1.2 Locally stored game data

Your game progress (completed levels, saved board drafts, hint counts, tutorial status, etc.) is stored only in local storage on your device and is never uploaded to our servers. You can delete this information at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data.

1.3 Advertising-related data

The Game integrates Google AdMob to serve rewarded video ads (e.g., hint restock, revive) and app open ads. AdMob and its partners may automatically collect the following information when serving ads:

  • Device information (device model, OS version, screen resolution, etc.)
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) and their settings
  • IP address and approximate location (e.g., country/city level)
  • Ad interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, etc.)

This data is collected and processed in accordance with Google's privacy policy. For details, see: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your system settings (typically: Settings → Google → Ads).

2. How Information Is Used

The information collected by us and our advertising provider is used solely to:

  • Serve and display ads (including verifying rewards from rewarded ads)
  • Keep the app running properly and securely (e.g., ad fraud prevention)
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use this information for any other purpose, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Information Sharing

We do not share your information with any third party except in the following cases:

  • Advertising provider: Google AdMob (Google LLC), for ad serving and reward verification;
  • Legal requirements: when required by applicable law or lawful requests from judicial authorities.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Security and Retention

  • Your game data is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game;
  • Data collected by the advertising provider is retained and processed under its own policy; see Google's privacy policy for details.

7. Your Rights

Depending on the applicable law in your region, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or opt out of ad personalization. Since the Game does not collect account-based personal information, most requests can be completed through your device settings (e.g., resetting your advertising ID or clearing app data).
